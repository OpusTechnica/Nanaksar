Our Heritage

17+ Years of Highway Soul & Sewa

From a humble highway stop on Dewas Naka in 2005 founded by Avtar Singh ("Veer Ji"), Nanaksar Dhaba has grown into Indore's iconic institution for authentic, honest, slow-cooked North Indian and Punjabi vegetarian cuisine.

The Spirit of "Sewa Bhaav"

Profit was never the primary objective. Founded on selfless hospitality, we ensure every guest experiences wholesome, comforting meals prepared with pure desi ghee, untouched hygiene, and heartwarming generosity.
